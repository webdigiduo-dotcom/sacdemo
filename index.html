Welcome to Sachika

Sachika Hotels is a contemporary four-star retreat near Guwahati, created for travellers who value comfort, connection, and thoughtful service.

The sign at our gate says atithi devo bhava: the guest is god. It’s a good place to start.

Rooms are ready from one o’clock and the front desk is open through the night, so a late flight or a long drive in is never a problem. We’re on the north bank of the Brahmaputra in Amingaon, beside IIT Guwahati and a short drive from AIIMS Guwahati.
